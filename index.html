Amazon Hunter Shorts Uploader

An internal tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel "아마존 사냥꾼" (Amazon Hunter) to schedule uploads of short videos to the owner's own channel and to review that channel's performance statistics.

This tool is not a public service and has no other users. It uses YouTube API Services.
